Asking where you are…

Keep it on your phone

iPhone: tap the Share button (square with an arrow) in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. One tap opens it any time.

Android: open the browser menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen.

Want it as a real iPhone widget?

The cat shows right on your Home Screen, with no tapping. It uses Scriptable, a free app. It takes about 3 minutes.

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store (free). Open in App Store
  2. Tap the blue button above to copy the code.
  3. Open Scriptable. Tap + (top right). Touch and hold the empty page, tap Paste. Tap the title at the top and name it Cloud Cat.
  4. Tap the ▶ play button. Allow location (Allow While Using App). You should see the cat.
  5. Close it. On your Home Screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap +, pick Scriptable, then Add Widget.
  6. Touch and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, set Script to Cloud Cat and When Interacting to Run Script.

Also on your Lock Screen

After the steps above: touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the box under the clock (or the row above it), choose Scriptable, and pick a shape. In Edit Widget, set Script to Cloud Cat. It shows a small cat emoji, the temperature and the weather. Needs iOS 16 or newer.

Tap the widget to switch °C / °F. It refreshes about every 20 minutes (iPhone decides the exact timing). Weather data: Open-Meteo. Your location stays on your phone.
